## Tips for Maximizing Heptabase for Visual Project Management

To truly harness Heptabase's power for visual project management, consider these practical tips:

1.  **Start with a "Project Hub" Map:** Create a main map for each major project. On this map, place cards linking to sub-maps for specific phases (e.g., "Research Phase Map," "Development Phase Map," "Marketing Plan Map"). This creates a hierarchical yet visual overview.
2.  **Use Cards for Everything:** Don't just think of cards as notes. Create cards for tasks, meeting agendas, key decisions, stakeholder contacts, and even potential risks. Link these cards to relevant sections of your project map.
3.  **Embrace Bi-directional Linking:** Whenever you create a new card that relates to an existing one, link them. This builds a robust knowledge graph. For example, link a task card to the research card that informs it, or link a meeting note card to the decision card that resulted from it.

5.  **Leverage the Journal for Daily Check-ins:** At the start or end of each day, use the Journal to log progress, identify blockers, and plan your immediate next steps. Link these journal entries directly to the relevant project cards or maps for context.
6.  **Color-Code for Clarity:** Use Heptabase's color-coding options for cards or groups of cards to visually distinguish different types of information (e.g., green for completed tasks, red for blockers, blue for research).
7.  **Create Custom Templates:** For recurring project types or common card structures (e.g., "Meeting Notes Template," "Bug Report Template"), create and save card templates to streamline your workflow.
8.  **Regularly Review and Refine Maps:** Just like a physical whiteboard, digital maps can get cluttered. Periodically review your project maps, archive old cards, refine connections, and reorganize for optimal clarity.
9.  **Integrate External Resources:** Embed links to external documents, cloud storage files, or web pages directly into cards. This makes your Heptabase map a central dashboard for all project-related assets.
