## How to Optimize Logseq for Long Form Writing

Choosing a theme is only the first step. Because Logseq is an outliner at its core, you must configure the [software](/posts/best-apps-for-personal-knowledge-management/) to accommodate prose.

### Disable the Outliner Visuals
When writing long paragraphs, the constant presence of bullet points breaks the illusion of a standard document. Use your theme's settings (or custom CSS via the `custom.css` file) to hide bullets for top-level blocks or when zooming into a specific node. 

### Implement Document Mode Plugins
To truly unlock Logseq for drafting, install the "Document Mode" or "Longform" plugins. These tools work alongside your theme to automatically hide UI elements, merge blocks visually into seamless paragraphs, and provide word count targets. Combine the Bear Theme with Document Mode, and Logseq becomes practically indistinguishable from Ulysses or iA Writer.

### Standardize Your Typography
For maximum readability, aim for a line height between 1.5 and 1.6, and restrict your line length (the maximum width of the text column) to between 65 and 80 characters. Wider text blocks force the eye to travel too far, causing fatigue. Most writing-focused themes like Awesome Styler allow you to enforce a maximum text width regardless of your monitor size.

## Conclusion

Transforming Logseq into a capable long-form writing environment requires softening its database-first interface. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Logseq really replace dedicated writing apps like Scrivener or Ulysses?
Yes, but with caveats. Logseq excels at integrating your research directly into your writing environment via block references. However, it lacks native compilation tools for exporting manuscripts to standard publishing formats (like ePub or formatted PDF), requiring third-party tools or pandoc for final publishing.

### How do I install a custom theme in Logseq?
Themes can be installed directly through Logseq's built-in plugin marketplace. Click the three dots in the top right, navigate to 'Plugins', select the 'Marketplace' tab, search for the theme name, and click install.

### How do I remove bullet points when writing in Logseq?
You can remove bullet points by using the keyboard shortcut `t d` (toggle document mode) in standard Logseq, which hides bullets and indent lines. For a permanent solution, many themes include custom CSS snippets to hide bullets entirely on specific pages or blocks.

### What is the best font to use for long-form writing in Logseq?
For body text, sans-serif fonts like Inter, system-ui, or Avenir Next reduce visual noise and improve legibility on screens. If you prefer serifs for drafting, Merriweather or Georgia are highly readable options that many custom themes support.
